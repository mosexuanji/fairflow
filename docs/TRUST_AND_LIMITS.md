# What the demonstration proves

The recorded local flow recognizes three controlled contributions, issues1175 FT, burns1 FT and retains1174 FT. Recognized contribution progress stays13.5 credits after the burn. A buyer-accepted1 LocalCash service order allocates0.7 to operations and0.3 to buyback; spending0.1 on the explicit local mock leaves0.2 in the buyback budget.

Tasks lock credits and cash rewards. FT is computed when each valid contribution is finalized. Under decreasing marginal issuance, finalization order can change an individual contributor's FT allocation; the cumulative issuance function preserves aggregate batch accounting. H1000 FT, Q10 credits, four role credits10/3/0.2/0.5,60-second review delay and70/30 allocation are hackathon-demo settings, not final public token economics.

Review uses controlled demo accounts and a designated reviewer. It is not independent third-party review, decentralized arbitration or a guarantee of contribution quality. An evidence digest proves unchanged bytes, not factual truth or universal semantic duplicate detection. A receipt is a nontransferable record; FT is not equity, stablecoin redemption or a guaranteed investment return.

LocalCash and mock liquidity are local fixtures. The static public-demo candidate contains a sanitized read-only recording of that local run, not live public-network balances. Arbitrum Sepolia deployment is prepared but not completed until actual signed transactions and explorer evidence exist. No mainnet, real liquidity pool, real swap, personal asset transfer, independent demand, production readiness or external audit is claimed.
